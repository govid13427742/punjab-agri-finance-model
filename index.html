Step 1 → 2

Farm to Processing

Raw produce moves to the village-block FPC within 5 km radius. No middlemen. The Sarpanch-led cooperative manages intake, quality checks, and processing scheduling.

₹20/kg raw wheat → ₹35/kg flour
Step 2 → 3

Processing to Packaging

Processed goods are packaged under the Punjab Agri-Pack brand with FSSAI certification. Quality labs at the district level ensure standards.

₹35/kg flour → ₹50/kg branded
Step 4 → 6

Market to Farmer

SMEs handle distribution and retail. Profits from the ₹30/kg value addition flow back to the farmer cooperative — not captured by middlemen.

Farmer gets ₹20/kg → Farmer gets ₹40+/kg

Critical Challenge

The Surplus Problem & Export Solution

Punjab produces 3-4x what its 3.1 crore population can consume. Without a demand pipeline for packaged goods, the entire value-addition model collapses. The answer lies in export corridors, diaspora markets, and institutional offtake.

Punjab's Production vs. Consumption Gap

Annual agricultural output far exceeds local demand — creating both a crisis and an opportunity

310 LMT Total Wheat + Rice Production
~80 LMT Local Consumption
230 LMT SURPLUS Requiring Export Channels
74% Production That Must Leave Punjab

The Multi-Channel Export & Absorption Strategy

Rather than depending on a single channel, the model creates 6 parallel demand pipelines that together can absorb the entire surplus as value-added products.

1
🇦🇪 🇸🇦 🇴🇲 🇲🇦 🇧🇭 🇴🇦

Middle East Food Security Corridor

Target: 80-100 LMT/year | $3-5 Billion

Why it works: Gulf states import 80-90% of their food. They have food security anxiety post-COVID. India is already their #1 food supplier. Punjab's packaged staples (rice, flour, dairy, pulses) are exactly what they need.

🇦🇪 UAE (Hub)
  • CEPA Free Trade Agreement already in force — zero/reduced tariffs on food
  • Dubai as re-export hub to East Africa, Central Asia
  • DMCC Food & Agri Commodity Centre for warehousing
  • 8 lakh+ Punjabi diaspora = instant retail demand
  • Punjab Agri-Pack brand in Lulu, Carrefour, Union Coop
🇸🇦 Saudi Arabia
  • Vision 2030 food security program actively seeking long-term supply partners
  • SALIC (Saudi Agricultural & Livestock Investment Co.) for G2G contracts
  • Basmati rice premium market — ₹80-120/kg retail
  • Institutional supply: Hajj/Umrah catering (20M+ pilgrims/year)
  • Military & government institutional kitchens
🇴🇲 🇲🇦 🇧🇭 Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman
  • Combined food import bill: $15+ Billion/year
  • Premium pricing for certified, traceable products
  • Long-term offtake contracts (3-5 year fixed price)
  • Halal certification via FSSAI + GCC Standardization Org
  • Qatar: FIFA legacy infrastructure = ongoing institutional demand

🇨🇦 🇬🇧 🇺🇸 🇦🇺

Punjabi Diaspora Premium Market

Target: 15-20 LMT/year | $1.5-2 Billion

Why it works: 3+ million Punjabis in Canada, UK, US, and Australia already buy Indian food products at 3-5x India prices. They want authentic Punjab products. Currently served by generic brands — Punjab Agri-Pack fills a massive emotional + quality gap.

🇨🇦 Canada 14 lakh Punjabis Atta: CAD $12/5kg (₹180/kg)
🇬🇧 United Kingdom 8 lakh Punjabis Basmati: £8/2kg (₹200/kg)
🇺🇸 United States 5 lakh Punjabis Dal: $6/2lb (₹275/kg)
🇦🇺 Australia 3 lakh Punjabis Ghee: AUD $18/kg (₹500/kg)
Punjab Agri-Pack products sold at diaspora prices generate 5-8x returns vs domestic sale. Even capturing 10% of diaspora grocery spend = ₹8,000+ Crores/year.
3
🌎

Government-to-Government Food Security Deals

Target: 50-70 LMT/year | $2-3 Billion

Why it works: Dozens of nations seek strategic grain reserves from reliable partners. India already has G2G rice/wheat deals with 20+ countries. Punjab can be the designated fulfilment state with value-added (fortified, packaged) products commanding premium pricing.

🌍 African Food Security

Nigeria, Ethiopia, Kenya, Tanzania, Mozambique — combined food import: $30B/year. Indian government already has LOIs. Punjab supplies fortified wheat flour & rice.

🌏 Southeast Asia

Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia, Bangladesh — periodic rice shortfalls. Pre-contracted emergency supply agreements at premium rates.

🗺 Strategic Reserve Contracts

Singapore, Japan, South Korea — maintain 90-180 day food reserves. Long-term (5-10 year) supply contracts with annual price escalation. Highest margin channel.

4
🏢✈🏪

Institutional & B2B Bulk Buyers

Target: 30-40 LMT/year | $1-2 Billion

Why it works: Airlines, hotel chains, cruise lines, military establishments, and school meal programs are massive, predictable buyers who sign annual contracts. Predictable demand = stable farmer income.

✈ Airlines

Indian carriers alone serve 50Cr+ meals/year. Ready-to-heat Punjab meals.

🏪 Hotel Chains

ITC, Taj, Marriott — bulk staple procurement. Certified origin = premium.

🎓 Mid-Day Meals

PM POSHAN scheme: 12 Cr children daily. Fortified Punjab flour & rice.

🛒 Retail Chains

Reliance, D-Mart, BigBasket — white-label + Punjab Agri-Pack branded.

5
🍲🍳

Ready-to-Eat & Ready-to-Cook (Global)

Target: 10-15 LMT/year | $2-3 Billion (high margin)

Why it works: The global RTE/RTC market is $280 Billion and growing 7% annually. Punjab's cuisine (dal makhani, rajma, sarson ka saag, paranthas) has global appeal. Japanese packaging technology (via existing partnership) enables 12-18 month shelf life. Highest margin channel: 300-500% over raw produce value.
